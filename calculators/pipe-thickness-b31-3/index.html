How to use this calculator

The calculator finds the wall a straight process pipe needs for internal pressure to ASME B31.3, adds the allowances and mill tolerance, and checks a schedule from ASME B36.10M. It also gives the lightest schedule that works, the MAWP of the chosen pipe and the hydrotest pressure. Every equation is shown with your values in it.

Steps

  1. Enter the design pressure and temperature of the line. Pick the pressure unit first.
  2. Choose the pipe size and a schedule. The outside diameter and nominal wall come from B36.10M. For other pipe, enter the OD and wall overrides.
  3. Enter the mill under-tolerance from the pipe specification (12.5% for A106 / A53) and the total allowance c.
  4. Enter the material allowable stress S from Table A-1M at design temperature, the quality factor E and the weld strength reduction factor W.
  5. Choose the material group. Y is read from Table 304.1.1 at your temperature, with interpolation between columns.
  6. Read the result. Step 4 compares the schedule wall, less mill tolerance, with tm; the table in step 5 shows every schedule for the size and marks those that pass.
  7. Enter ST to get the hydrotest pressure, then print or save as PDF.

Good practice

  • The equation is valid for t < D/6 and P/(SE) ≤ 0.385. Beyond that, 304.1.2(b) calls for special consideration (theory of failure, fatigue, thermal stress): the calculator flags it.
  • Use the allowable stress at the design temperature, and the lowest value along the line if conditions vary.
  • Include thread or groove depth in c for threaded or grooved pipe.
  • Check the test stress against yield during hydrotest (345.4.2(c)), especially at high design temperature where ST/S is large.
  • Pipe dimensions here are from ASME B36.10M; verify them against your copy and use B36.19M for stainless 5S and 10S walls.

Not covered

  • External pressure (304.1.3), pipe bends and miters (304.2), branch reinforcement (304.3), flanges and other components.
