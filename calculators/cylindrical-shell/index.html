How to use this calculator

The calculator checks a cylindrical shell against ASME VIII-1: required thickness for internal pressure, MAWP and MAP new and cold, external pressure, MDMT and impact testing, PWHT, forming strain and hydrotest pressure. Every equation is shown with your values in it, so the result can be checked line by line.

Steps

  1. Enter the dimensions. Inside diameter, tangent length, nominal thickness, under-tolerance and corrosion allowance. The design length L is used only for external pressure. Choose vertical or horizontal: it sets the liquid head used for the static pressure.
  2. Enter the material and design conditions. Joint efficiencies to UW-12, the fluid specific gravity for the static head, and the design and hydrotest temperatures.
  3. Enter the material strengths from ASME Section II Part D. Allowable stress at design and test temperature. Yield and modulus are needed only for some external-pressure cases; the page highlights them when they are.
  4. Enter the design pressures. Pick the pressure unit first. Internal pressure is at the top of the vessel; the static head is added for the bottom. Choose none, full vacuum or another external pressure.
  5. For external pressure, read the chart factors. Step 4 of the report shows L/Do and Do/t. Use them to read factor A from Fig. G and factor B from the material chart in Section II Part D, Subpart 3, then enter A and B. If A falls to the left of the material curve, tick the box and enter the modulus instead of B.
  6. For carbon and low-alloy steel, complete the MDMT section. MDMT from Fig. UCS-66 for the material curve, the UCS-66.1 reduction, the optional UCS-68(c) reduction, UG-20(f) if all its conditions are met, and the required service MDMT.
  7. Tick the PWHT reasons that apply and enter the forming limits (allowable fibre elongation and, for re-rolled plate, the original radius).
  8. Read the result. The banner turns green when the shell is accepted, with the thickness margin. Each step in the report has its own PASS / FAIL or note. If the shell fails, increase the nominal thickness (and, for external pressure, read A and B again).
  9. Print or save as PDF to keep the calculation with your inputs.

Good practice

  • Use the allowable stresses from the Section II Part D edition that applies to the vessel, at the design temperature of the shell.
  • Under-tolerance: UG-16(c) lets plate under-tolerance up to the lesser of 0.25 mm or 6% be ignored. For pipe, take 12.5% of the nominal thickness.
  • The external-pressure check is iterative: after changing the thickness, read A and B again for the new Do/t.
  • For hydrotest, use the lowest stress ratio of all the pressure parts, not only the shell.
  • Inputs are kept in your browser, so the page reopens with your last case. Use Clear inputs to start a new one.

Not covered

  • Thick shells beyond the UG-27 limits (Mandatory Appendix 1-2): the page flags this but does not calculate it.
  • Stiffening ring design, nozzle reinforcement (tr is given for use in UG-37), heads, cones, wind, seismic and other loads.
  • Impact test rules for non-UCS materials (UHA-51, UNF-65 and others).
