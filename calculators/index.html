Engineering calculators

Free calculators for pressure equipment, welding, materials and inspection checks. Each shows the input parameters and units, the equations with your values substituted, results with units, the code reference and its assumptions and limitations. They are aids for engineering work, not complete code-compliance assessments. Code-tabulated values such as allowable stresses and external-pressure factors are entered by you from the code edition you use.
