How to use this calculator

The calculator checks a formed head for a pressure vessel against ASME VIII-1. It works in the corroded condition, allows for the thinning that happens when the plate is pressed or spun into a head, and gives the required thickness, MAWP, hydrotest pressure and, if needed, the external pressure check. Every equation is shown with your values in it.

Steps

  1. Choose the head type. For a 2:1 ellipsoidal or a standard F&D torispherical head, only the skirt inside diameter is needed: the head proportions are set automatically. For a general ellipsoidal head, enter the inside depth h; for a general torispherical head, the crown radius L and knuckle radius r.
  2. Enter the nominal plate thickness and the forming thinning from the head manufacturer, and the corrosion allowance.
  3. Enter the material allowable stresses at design and test temperature from Section II Part D, and the joint efficiency.
  4. Enter the internal pressure at the head. Pick the pressure unit first.
  5. For external pressure, choose full vacuum or another value. Step 6 shows factor A: read factor B from the material chart in Section II Part D and enter it. If A falls to the left of the curve, tick the box and enter the modulus instead.
  6. Read the result. The banner turns green only when the thickness, the formula limits, the MAWP and (if any) the external pressure checks all pass. Step 3 gives the nominal plate thickness needed if the check fails.
  7. Print or save as PDF to keep the calculation with your inputs.

Good practice

  • Order the plate thick enough to cover thinning: the minimum thickness after forming, not the plate thickness, must meet t + CA. The head manufacturer should guarantee the minimum after forming.
  • For the same pressure and diameter, a hemispherical head needs about half the wall of a 2:1 ellipsoidal head, and a standard F&D head about 1.75 times.
  • Torispherical heads must have a knuckle radius of at least 6% of the skirt OD and at least 3 times the head thickness, and a crown radius not larger than the skirt OD.
  • For hydrotest, use the lowest stress ratio St/S of all the pressure parts.
  • The external-pressure check is iterative: after changing the thickness, read B again at the new factor A.

Not covered

  • Conical, toriconical and flat heads; forming strain and heat treatment after forming (UG-79, UCS-79); nozzles in the knuckle; very thin heads needing Appendix 1-4(f).
