How to use this calculator

Local PWHT heats only a band around the weld, so the heated and insulated areas must be wide enough to bring the whole soak band, through the wall, to temperature without harmful gradients. ASME VIII-1 sets the soak band but gives no size for the heated band or the insulation; WRC 452 and AWS D10.10 do. The calculator gives all three bands, compares them with PD 5500 / AS 1210, and for P-No. 1 material gives the UCS-56 holding time and heating and cooling rates. The background is explained in the article Local PWHT for Pressure Vessels and Piping.

Steps

  1. Enter the outside diameter and thickness of the shell, pipe or nozzle at the weld, and the widest width of the finished weld.
  2. Choose the P-Number group and enter the minimum soak temperature from the code table or procedure.
  3. Read the bands. Each is centred on the weld; step 3 also gives the distance from the weld centreline to each edge, for marking the component.
  4. Compare with PD 5500 / AS 1210 in step 4. Where the vessel code gives no band size, using the wider of the two is a prudent choice.
  5. For P-No. 1, read the minimum holding time and the maximum heating and cooling rates above 425 °C in step 5.
  6. Print or save as PDF and attach it to the PWHT procedure.

Good practice

  • The heated band controls the through-wall temperature difference; the gradient control band (insulation) controls the axial gradient. Keep the temperature at the edge of the heated band at least half the soak temperature.
  • Place thermocouples at the weld centreline and at the soak band edges on the outside surface, and on the inside surface where accessible.
  • Support the component so that the heated section can expand and does not sag; poor local PWHT can distort a vessel.
  • For Grade 91 and other P-No. 15E steels, ASME Section I Appendix VIII and ASME B31P Appendix B have their own band and temperature rules; check the filler metal Ni + Mn limits too.
  • For nozzles, heads and other non-cylindrical geometry, or welds near structural discontinuities, consider FEA of the heating arrangement.

Not covered

  • Holding times and rates for P-Numbers other than P-No. 1, band sizes of ASME Section I and B31P, thermocouple and heater layout, and spot or bulls-eye PWHT.
