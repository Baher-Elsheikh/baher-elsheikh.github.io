About

I'm Baher Elsheikh. I have 25 years of experience with static equipment in petrochemical, methanol and refining plants, with a focus on risk-based inspection, damage mechanisms and steam reformer integrity management.

I built this site on one idea: knowledge grows when it is communicated. It shares practical knowledge from that work, free for engineers at every stage, from students to experienced specialists. The calculators started as my own spreadsheets; each one shows its equations step by step and is checked against a worked example before it is published.

I have written more than 11 technical publications, in outlets including Inspectioneering Journal, Heat Exchanger World, Stainless Steel World, NACE and the ASME Pressure Vessels and Piping Conference. Selected articles are listed under Articles.
